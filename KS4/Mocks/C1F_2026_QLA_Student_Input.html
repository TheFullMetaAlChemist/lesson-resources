Using this walkthrough

Use this after your mock has been returned. Choose a question part, compare your answer with the model answer, then open the explanation or tips if you need them.

  • Model answer shows a full-mark response. The marking checklist lets you tick the points that are in your own answer.
  • Why it works explains the chemistry step by step.
  • Exam tips covers wording and common errors. For calculations, Check your answer lists wrong answers students often get and what caused them.
  • Try another gives a fresh question with a hint and a revealable answer.
  • My results is where you enter your mark for each part. You get a breakdown by topic, question type and assessment objective.
  • Your two focus questions are picked automatically from the parts where you dropped marks in your weakest topic and question type. Type your answers on the results page; they are printed on your results sheet.

Assessment objectives are the three skills the exam tests. AO1 is knowledge and understanding: remembering facts, key words and methods. AO2 is applying knowledge: using ideas in new situations, including calculations. AO3 is analysing and evaluating: interpreting data and graphs, drawing conclusions and improving experiments.

Print my results sheet prints one double-sided sheet of A4: your analysis on the front and your focus questions on the back. Choose “Print on both sides” in the print settings.

Only your ticks, review status, marks, name, focus answers and text-size preference are saved, in this browser on this device. No account or internet connection is needed. If browser storage is blocked, they may not be kept between visits.

Use A+ to enlarge the text. Choose Enlarge diagram for a closer view of a diagram. Keyboard users can move between the support tabs using the left and right arrow keys.

Questions in this mock are taken from an AQA GCSE Combined Science: Trilogy examination paper (8464/C/1F, June 2026), used for internal teaching and revision. With acknowledgement to AQA.
