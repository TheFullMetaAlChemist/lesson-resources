Using this walkthrough

Use this after your mock has been returned. Choose a question part, compare your answer with the model answer, then open the explanation or tips if you need them.

  • Model answer shows a full-mark response. The marking checklist lets you tick the points that are in your own answer.
  • Why it works explains the chemistry step by step.
  • Exam tips covers wording and common errors. For calculations, Check your answer lists wrong answers students often get and what caused them.
  • Try another gives a fresh question with a hint and a revealable answer. Answer on paper.
  • My results is where you enter your mark for each part. It shows your strongest and weakest topics and question types and which parts to revisit first. You can also record marks in the box under each question.

The paper has two questions numbered 04. They are labelled 04 (Group 7) and 04 (Atmosphere) here, and in your results as 04.1 (G7), 04.1 (Atm) and so on. There are no questions 05 or 07 on this paper.

Only your ticks, review status, marks and text-size preference are saved, in this browser on this device. No account or internet connection is needed. If browser storage is blocked, they may not be kept between visits.

Use A+ to enlarge the text. Choose Enlarge diagram for a closer view of a diagram. Keyboard users can move between the support tabs using the left and right arrow keys.

Questions in this mock are taken from past AQA GCSE Chemistry examination papers, used for internal teaching and revision. With acknowledgement to AQA. Model answers, marking points, explanations and practice questions are teacher-written.
