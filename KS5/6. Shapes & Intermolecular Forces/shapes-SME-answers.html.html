Explain a property

Structure → attraction → energy → property. Name the particles and the forces between them; compare the forces and link to the energy needed to separate the particles.

Polarity: identify polar bonds, state the geometry, then decide whether the bond dipoles cancel.

Solubility: compare attractions disrupted with solute–solvent attractions formed.

Melting: include crystal packing where necessary; do not simply copy a boiling-point argument.
