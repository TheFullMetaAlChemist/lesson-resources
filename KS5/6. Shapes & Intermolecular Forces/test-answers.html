Using this walkthrough

Use this after attempting the test on paper. Choose a sub-question, compare your work with the model answer, then open the explanation or tips if you need them.

  • Model answer shows a concise response and completed diagrams. The marking checklist lets you tick the points present in your own answer.
  • Why it works explains the chemistry in manageable steps.
  • Exam tips highlights wording and common errors.
  • Try another gives a fresh question with a hint and a revealable answer. Answer on paper.

Checklist ticks are for self-marking; this guide does not automatically assess written answers. Only ticks, review status and text-size preference are saved in this browser. No account or internet connection is needed. If browser storage is blocked, ticks may not persist.

Use A+ to enlarge the answer text. Choose Enlarge diagram for a closer view of a diagram. Keyboard users can move between the support tabs using the left and right arrow keys.

This is a teacher-written companion to the 40-mark Topic 6 assessment. The marking requirements follow its supplied mark scheme. Questions 2(b–c) adapt OCR H432/01 June 2017 Q16(b); 5(b) adapts OCR H032/02 November 2020 Q3(c)(ii). The other questions are teacher-written.
